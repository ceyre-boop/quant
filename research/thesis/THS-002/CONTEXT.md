## Mechanism (who is forced to pay, and why)
2026 hyperscaler capex is ~$700B+ and guidance has risen each quarter; HBM/DRAM and accelerators
are sold out through 2027. The buildout is now limited by electricity: grid interconnection takes
24–36 months, transformer and turbine backlogs run to 2028, and US generation capacity has been
roughly flat for 15 years. Every data center must buy turbines, switchgear, grid construction and
firm power whatever chip or model wins. Supply of those cannot be expanded quickly, so the sellers
get pricing power for the horizon of this thesis.

GEV 25% · ETN 20% · CEG 15% · VST 15% · PWR 15% · FIX 10%. No overlap with THS-001's sealed names

## Invalidation
Mechanical: basket drawdown ≥30% from running peak. Manual: ≥2 of MSFT/GOOGL/AMZN/META cut forward
capex guidance two consecutive quarters. Printed as a question at each scoring run.

## Why this and not more compute
Semis are the most stretched vs their 200-day average since 2000 and THS-001 already holds that
leg. Power names price the same demand with a slower, contract-backed revenue path.
